HOW TO READ THIS DATA

Published signals describe configuration at scan time.

SPF and DMARC show published email policies. Website headers, TLS certificate details, HTTPS redirects, and security.txt are sampled with bounded public GET and HEAD requests. DNSSEC distinguishes secure, unsigned, broken, and lookup-error results. DKIM checks common selectors only, so no match is inconclusive. Provider names are clues inferred from published hostnames and headers.
