HOW TO READ THIS DATA

Published policy is a signal, not a message test.

SPF qualifiers describe the record’s default sender treatment; a valid record without an explicit ending evaluates to neutral. DMARC shows the requested policy and where reports are sent. DNS alone cannot show whether a particular email passed, and provider labels are inferred from published hostnames.
